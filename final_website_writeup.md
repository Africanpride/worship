# THE VISION

**Recovering, Reviving & Restoring the Tabernacle of David**

**"They never rest day and night..."**

\- Revelation 4:8

The 144 Hours Non-Stop Praise, Worship, Prayer & Bible Reading Series is
a kingdom-focused movement dedicated to establishing on earth a
reflection of the continuous worship that takes place before the throne
of God in Heaven.

Under the leadership of the Logos-Rhema Foundation for Leadership
Resource Development (LRF), the Non-Stop Series began in 2002 as a
gathering for 24 hours of continuous praise and worship. Over the years,
through divine direction and growth, the altar has expanded
progressively from 24 to 48, to 72, 96, 120 and now 144 continuous hours
-- a full week -- of concurrent praise, worship, prayer, and Bible
reading.

This journey represents more than an increase in duration. It reflects a
growing call to raise an altar of unbroken service before the Lord - an
altar where worship ascends continuously day and night; 'twenty-four
seven'.

**Our Foundation**

The vision of the Non-Stop Series is rooted in the understanding that
worship is central to God's eternal purpose.

Jesus taught His disciples to pray:

"Your will be done on earth as it is in Heaven."

In Heaven, worship never ceases.

Revelation 4:8 declares:

"They never rest day and night, saying: Holy, holy, holy, Lord God
Almighty."

The Non-Stop Series is therefore a passionate pursuit to reflect on
earth what is already taking place continually in Heaven - unending
worship, adoration, thanksgiving, prayer, and the exaltation of God.

**The Tabernacle of David**

**Recovering, Reviving & Restoring the Tabernacle of David**

Throughout Scripture, God revealed different expressions of worship and
approaches to His presence. Yet among all the earthly altars and
tabernacles described in the Bible, the Tabernacle of David stands apart
as the clearest reflection of the worship that continually rises before
the throne of God in heaven.

Unlike the Tabernacle of Moses, where access to God\'s presence was
restricted by successive courts and veils, David\'s tabernacle was
distinguished by two defining characteristics:

- It removed the barriers that separated God\'s people from His
  manifested presence, allowing direct, continual access before the Ark
  of the Covenant.

- It established an ordered system of continual praise, worship,
  thanksgiving, prayer, and musical ministry, with singers, musicians,
  and worshippers ministering before the Lord, day and night.

It is this biblical model that inspires our vision.

The vision of the Non-Stop Praise, Worship, Prayer & Bible Reading
Series is to recover, revive, and restore the spirit of the Tabernacle
of David in our generation.

- To ***recover*** is to return to the biblical pattern of worship that
  God established through David.

- To ***revive*** is to awaken and strengthen a fresh passion for
  continual worship, prayer, and devotion among God\'s people.

- To ***restore*** is to bring this model once again to the forefront of
  the Church\'s life and culture, so that worship is not merely an
  activity confined to services or special events but becomes a
  continuous offering unto the Lord.

King David cultivated a culture of uninterrupted ministry before God\'s
presence. He appointed skilled musicians, singers, gatekeepers, and
leaders to minister continually with songs, instruments, thanksgiving,
prayer, and declarations of God\'s greatness. Worship was not an
occasional event; it became the rhythm of the nation and a continual
offering before the Lord.

Our 144 Hours Non-Stop Praise, Worship, Prayer & Bible Reading Series is
a contemporary expression of that same vision. While we do not claim to
recreate David\'s tabernacle in its historical form, we seek to embody
its spirit by establishing an altar where praise, worship, prayer,
thanksgiving, and the public reading of Scripture rise continuously
before God.

Our prayer is that this vision will extend beyond one annual gathering
and inspire homes, churches, campuses, communities, cities, and nations
to establish their own altars of continual worship, until the sound of
heaven is increasingly reflected on the earth.

King David established a culture of continual worship before the
presence of God through music, poetry, proclamations, prayer,
thanksgiving, and praise. Skilled musicians, singers, worshippers, and
gatekeepers ministered before the Lord continually.

David understood how to touch the heart of God.

Scripture describes him as:

"A man after My own heart." - Acts 13:22

The Tabernacle of David represented more than a physical tent. It
represented a people gathered around the presence of God in continual
worship.

The prophets foretold its restoration:

"On that day I will raise up the tabernacle of David which has fallen
down." - Amos 9:11

And in Acts 15:16--17, this restoration was connected to the gathering
of nations unto the Lord.

The Non-Stop Series exists as part of this prophetic restoration:

- To recover the original pattern of continuous worship;

- To revive authentic praise and worship in spirit and truth;

- To restore worship to its rightful place at the centre of individual
  lives, corporate ministries, business leadership, and nations.

**Why 144 Hours?**

The progression from 24 hours to 144 hours reflects a continual upward
journey in obedience to divine leading. 144 hours is exactly one week --
7 days -- the basic cycle of divine order. The timeframe presents a
statement of completion and perfection, and of the rhythmic balance
between labour and rest.

Accordingly, the 144-hour altar represents:

- Continuous ministry unto the Lord;

- Perpetual rest in His Presence;

- Sustained prayer and intercession for nations;

- The unity of worshippers across generations and nations;

- A prophetic declaration that the fire on the altar must never go out.

Numerologically, the sum of 24 (representative of the 24 elders
constantly leading worship in heaven) and 120 (representative of
priesthood & the restoration of the governing presence of God to man --
the number of days grace given for man to repent before God sent the
flood in Gen 6:3; the number of priests assigned to assist Ezra restore
Gods word after return from Babylonian captivity according to Jewish
historical tradition; the number of priests assigned to lead worship
when the ark was returned to Jerusalem and into Solomons temple at its
dedication in 2 Chron 5:12; the exact number of Christian believers that
waited for the manifestation of the holy spirit after Jesus' departure
in Acts 1:15) is 144. ***The number thus also represents the ultimate
desired outcome of the Non-stop Series: priesthood on earth that mirrors
heavenly priesthood.***

The convocation is also deliberately programmed to begin at 6:00 PM on
the first day and conclude at 6:00 PM on the final day, following the
biblical pattern established in Genesis, where God reckoned the day from
evening to evening.

"So, he left Asaph and his brothers there before the ark of the covenant

A watch is a dedicated period of continuous ministry unto the Lord.

Each watch becomes:

- A moment of worship;

- A time of intercession;

- A spiritual assignment carried by worshippers and watchmen.

Throughout the 144 hours, the altar remains active continuously with

- Praise & Worship

- Prayer & Intercession

before the Lord.

"They never rest day and night..." - Revelation 4:8

**ABOUT THE MOVEMENT**

**More Than an Event. A Movement of Continuous Worship.**

The Non-Stop Praise, Worship, Prayer & Bible Reading Series is a
kingdom-focused movement committed to recovering, reviving, and
restoring the Tabernacle of David in our generation.

For over two decades, this movement has sought to establish on earth a
reflection of the continuous worship that takes place before the throne
of God in Heaven.

What began as a single 24-hour gathering has grown into a 144-hour altar
of uninterrupted praise, worship, prayer, and Scripture reading,
bringing together worshippers, churches, ministries, leaders, musicians,
intercessors, and believers from across nations.

At its heart, the movement exists for one purpose:

To glorify God continually and to help restore worship to its rightful
place at the centre of life, ministry, leadership, and nations.

**Our Story**

The Non-Stop Series was initiated in 2002 under the leadership of Rev.
Dr. Abu Bako through the Logos-Rhema Foundation for Leadership Resource
Development (LRF).

From the beginning, the vision was simple yet profound: to create an
opportunity for believers to engage on earth in the one activity that
continually takes place in Heaven - worship before the Lord.

Inspired by Revelation 4:8, where worship around God\'s throne never
ceases day or night, the movement began with a 24-hour continuous praise
and worship gathering.

Over the years, the altar has expanded progressively:

- 24 Hours (2002--2013)

- 48 Hours (2014)

- 72 Hours (2015)

- 96 Hours (2016--2018)

- 120 Hours (2019--2020)

- 144 Hours (2021--Present)

Each increase represents an ongoing journey of obedience, growth, and
commitment to sustaining continuous worship before the Lord.

**Recover. Revive. Restore.**

The movement is built around a threefold mandate:

**Recover**

To recover the original biblical understanding of worship as a lifestyle
and continual ministry unto the Lord.

To recover the pattern established by David, where worshippers
ministered before God\'s presence continuously with songs, instruments,
thanksgiving, declarations, and prayer.

**Revive**

To awaken fresh passion, devotion, and hunger for God\'s presence.

To inspire a new generation of worshippers, intercessors, musicians, and
leaders who understand that worship is central to God\'s purposes.

**Restore**

To restore worship, prayer, and the Word to their rightful place within
individuals, families, churches, communities, and nations.

To help rebuild the spiritual foundations necessary for revival,
transformation, and lasting impact.

**The Tabernacle of David**

The constant theme of the movement is:

**Recovering, Reviving & Restoring the Tabernacle of David**

The Tabernacle of David represented a culture of continual worship
before the presence of God.

King David established worshippers, singers, musicians, and ministers
who served before the Lord continually.

The prophets later declared that God would restore this tabernacle.

"On that day I will raise up the tabernacle of David which has fallen
down."\
- Amos 9:11

The movement embraces this prophetic call by encouraging continuous
worship, prayer, Scripture reading, and thanksgiving as expressions of
devotion unto God.

We believe that when worship is restored, hearts are transformed,
communities are strengthened, and nations are drawn closer to God\'s
purposes.

**A Gathering of Nations**

Since its inception, the Non-Stop Series has welcomed ministers,
worshippers, and participants from numerous nations across Africa and
the world.

Voices, languages, cultures, and worship expressions have come together
on one altar with one purpose: to glorify the Lord.

This reflects the biblical vision of nations worshipping together before
God and fulfills our desire to see people from every tribe, tongue, and
nation united in praise.

**The Four Continuous Expressions**

The movement is distinguished by four simultaneous expressions that
continue throughout the duration of the altar:

**Praise & Worship**

Continuous adoration, thanksgiving, and exaltation of God.

**Prayer & Intercession**

Standing in the gap for individuals, communities, and nations.

**Bible Reading**

The continual public declaration of God\'s Word.

**Thanksgiving & Testimony**

Celebrating God\'s goodness, faithfulness, and mighty acts.

Together, these expressions create an atmosphere that seeks to mirror
Heaven\'s continuous worship.

**Looking Ahead**

The vision extends beyond a single annual gathering.

We believe the Lord is calling for the establishment of continuous
worship and prayer in cities, regions, and nations.

Our prayer is that the Non-Stop Series will continue to grow from a
programme-based initiative into a global movement of worshippers and

We envision:

# START AN ALTAR PAGE

*Note: This page moves the Non-Stop Series from being an annual event to
becoming a multiplying movement. The message should be:*

***\"Don\'t just attend the altar. Build an altar.\"***

*The goal is to empower families, churches (community assemblies),
campuses, ministries, workplaces, communities, cities, and nations to
establish their own expressions of continuous praise, worship, prayer,
and Scripture reading.*

**START AN ALTAR PAGE**

**Take The Vision to Your Sphere of Influence**

The Non-Stop Praise, Worship, Prayer & Bible Reading Series is more than
a gathering.

It is a call to establish altars of worship, prayer, and God\'s Word
wherever He has placed us.

While the annual Non-Stop Series brings thousands together for 144
continuous hours, we believe the vision extends far beyond a single
event, location, or nation.

Our desire is to see families, churches, campuses, ministries,
communities, workplaces, cities, and nations establishing their own
expressions of continuous worship and prayer.

You do not need to start with 144 hours.

Start where you are.

Start with what you have.

Start with those around you.

The important thing is to begin.

**The Vision Is Multiplication**

The Tabernacle of David was not merely about one tent in Jerusalem.

It represented a culture of continual worship before the Lord.

We believe God is calling His people to establish that culture in every
sphere of influence.

Imagine:

- A family gathering for 3 hours of worship and Scripture reading.

- A church hosting a 6-hour prayer and worship watch.

- A campus fellowship sustaining a 12-hour altar.

- A community organizing a 24-hour worship gathering.

- A city joining together for 72 hours of prayer and worship.

- Nations establishing continuous altars before the Lord.

Every altar matters.

Every watch matters.

Every gathering contributes to a larger sound of worship rising before
God.

**Start Where You Are**

You do not need a large budget.

You do not need a large venue.

You do not need famous worship leaders.

You simply need willing hearts gathered around God\'s presence.

Whether you are:

- A family;

- A small group;

- A church;

- A prayer ministry;

- A campus fellowship;

- A worship team;

- A youth movement;

- A community network;

you can begin.

**Suggested Models**

**Family Altar**

**Duration:** 1--3 Hours

Include:

- Worship

- Prayer

- Bible Reading

- Thanksgiving

Perfect for families and close friends.

**Community Altar**

**Duration:** 3--6 Hours

Bring together:

- Families

- Neighbours

- Local churches

- Prayer groups

Focus on:

- Community transformation

- Healing

- Revival

- Unity

**Church Worship Watch**

**Duration:** 6--12 Hours

Organize rotating teams for:

- Worship

- Prayer

- Scripture Reading

Great for monthly or quarterly gatherings.

**City Altar**

**Duration:** 12--24 Hours

Bring churches and ministries together in one continuous expression of
worship and prayer.

Focus on:

- Unity

- Revival

- Transformation

**Regional & National Altars**

**Duration:** 24 Hours and Beyond

Mobilize churches, ministries, worship teams, and intercessors to
sustain continuous worship and prayer across a city, region, or nation.

**The Four Expressions**

Whatever the duration, we encourage every altar to include:

**Praise & Worship**

Exalting and glorifying God.

**Prayer & Intercession**

Standing in the gap for people and nations.

**Bible Reading**

Publicly declaring God\'s Word.

**Thanksgiving & Testimony**

Celebrating God\'s faithfulness.

These four expressions form the foundation of the Non-Stop vision.

#### 3. THE VISION

- Main Title: The Vision
- Subtitle: Recovering, Reviving & Restoring the Tabernacle of David
- Key Verse: "They never rest day and night..." — Revelation 4:8
- Overview:
  The 144 Hours Non-Stop Praise, Worship, Prayer & Bible Reading Series is a kingdom-focused movement dedicated to establishing on earth a reflection of the continuous worship that takes place before the throne of God in Heaven.
  Under the leadership of the Logos-Rhema Foundation for Leadership Resource Development (LRF), the Non-Stop Series began in 2002 as a gathering for 24 hours of continuous praise and worship. Over the years, through divine direction and growth, the altar has expanded progressively from 24 to 48, to 72, 96, 120 and now 144 continuous hours — a full week — of concurrent praise, worship, prayer, and Bible reading.
  This journey represents more than an increase in duration. It reflects a growing call to raise an altar of unbroken service before the Lord — an altar where worship ascends continuously day and night; 'twenty-four seven'.
- Core Structural Elements to Render:
  - Our Foundation: Jesus' teaching ("Your will be done on earth as it is in Heaven") & Revelation 4:8.
  - The Tabernacle of David: Contrast Tabernacle of Moses vs. Tabernacle of David (removal of barriers, direct access before the Ark, ordered system of continual praise, worship, prayer, and musical ministry day and night).
  - The Three-Fold Mandate:
    - RECOVER: Return to the biblical pattern of worship established through David.
    - REVIVE: Awaken and strengthen a fresh passion for continual worship, prayer, and devotion.
    - RESTORE: Bring this model back to the forefront of Church life and culture as a continuous offering.
  - Prophetic Alignment: Amos 9:11 & Acts 15:16–17 (gathering of nations).
  - "Why 144 Hours?" Breakdown:
    - 7 days divine order & completion.
    - Perpetual rest in His Presence & sustained intercession.
    - Numerology: 24 elders + 120 (priesthood & governing presence from Gen 6:3, Ezra, 2 Chron 5:12, Acts 1:15) = 144. Represents priesthood on earth mirroring heaven.
    - Evening-to-Evening Cycle: Programmed 6:00 PM to 6:00 PM per Genesis.
  - A Global Altar: Highlighting nations (Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa, Rwanda, Ethiopia, Benin, Namibia, Israel, Egypt, Ivory Coast, Bahamas, Switzerland, France, USA, etc. / Acts 17:27).
  - More Than an Event: Living altar, call to consecration, gathering of watchmen, school of worship.
  - Four Simultaneous Expressions: Praise & Worship | Prayer & Intercession | Bible Reading | Thanksgiving & Testimony.
